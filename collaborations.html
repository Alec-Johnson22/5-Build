Collaborations

Connect with other creatives to bring ideas to life.

Indie Film Collective believes that great films are built through collaboration. We work with local filmmakers, artists, musicians, actors, students, businesses, and community organizations to create opportunities for people to share ideas and bring original stories to life. Through collaborative film projects, workshops, special screenings, guest speakers, and community partnerships, we connect creative individuals and help strengthen the independent film community.
